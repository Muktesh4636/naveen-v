Privacy Policy – Tik Tik

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Tik Tik is a Chrome extension that helps you find and book US visa appointment slots on usvisascheduling.com. This policy explains what data the extension uses and why.

Data the extension uses

  • Account details you enter (portal username, password, security answers) – stored only in your browser's local extension storage, used to sign you back in to usvisascheduling.com when you are logged out.
  • Your booking preferences (cities, date range, settings) – stored in your browser and linked to your account on the.gopg.online.
  • Activity logs (dates found, cities checked, Submit clicks, errors, extension version) – sent to the.gopg.online about every 10 minutes so we can support you and fix problems.
  • Notifications – when a Submit is clicked, a short message may be sent to your Telegram through our server if you set it up.

How the data is used

Data is used only to provide the extension's features: checking appointment dates, booking, alerts, sign-in, and support. We do not sell or rent your data, do not use it for advertising, and do not share it with third parties except as needed to deliver Telegram alerts you requested.

Where data is stored

Passwords and security answers stay in your browser. Preferences and activity logs are stored on our server at the.gopg.online and are accessible only to our support team.

Your choices

You can remove saved details in the extension popup, or uninstall the extension at any time to stop all data collection. To delete data held on our server, contact us and we will remove it.

Contact

Questions: visit https://the.gopg.online.
